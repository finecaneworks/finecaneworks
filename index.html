A LOCAL WORKSHOP, A LASTING CRAFT

Made with patience.
Meant to be lived with.

Over 15 years of bringing the natural warmth of cane into everyday spaces.

In Parempadam, Kerala, Fine Cane Works is a small workshop where cane is shaped and woven into useful, beautiful pieces for the home.

For over 15 years, we have worked with this versatile natural material—creating furniture and home accessories with care for the details, the finish and the way each piece will be used.

From a chair for a quiet corner to a custom piece made for your space, every project starts with a conversation. We also help repair and polish much-loved cane furniture.

THE BEAUTY IS IN THE DETAILS

A natural material.
A human touch.

Every piece begins with cane, careful hands and an idea worth making.

Cane has a unique character. Lightweight yet strong, natural yet versatile, it can be transformed into everything from functional furniture to delicate decorative pieces.

At Fine Cane Works, our skilled craftsmen work with cane using techniques developed through years of experience.

  • ✓ Shaping and weaving cane
  • ✓ Creating furniture frames and structures
  • ✓ Detailed cane work and finishing
  • ✓ Custom-designed furniture
  • ✓ Decorative and functional accessories
  • ✓ Repair and restoration of old cane furniture

Every handcrafted piece has its own character, and the small variations that come with handmade work are part of what makes cane furniture special.
