About Fine Cane Works

Over 15 Years of Craftsmanship

Located in the heart of Parempadam, Kerala, Fine Cane Works is a one-craftsman workshop creating beautiful, handwoven cane furniture with traditional techniques passed down through generations.

For more than 15 years, we have been creating and supplying cane furniture and home accessories for customers looking for products that are practical, distinctive and beautifully crafted.

Our in-house cane craftsmen bring years of experience to every piece, from shaping and weaving cane to the finishing touches. This allows us to maintain close attention to the quality and workmanship of our products.

Whether you are looking for a functional piece of furniture, a distinctive addition to your home, a custom-made design, or a traditional cane piece that needs to be restored/polished, we are here to help.

Our Craft

Where Traditional Skill Meets Everyday Design

Cane has a unique character. Lightweight yet strong, natural yet versatile, it can be transformed into everything from functional furniture to delicate decorative pieces.

At Fine Cane Works, our skilled craftsmen work with cane using techniques developed through years of experience.

  • ✓ Shaping and weaving cane
  • ✓ Creating furniture frames and structures
  • ✓ Detailed cane work and finishing
  • ✓ Custom-designed furniture
  • ✓ Decorative and functional accessories
  • ✓ Repair and restoration of old cane furniture

Every handcrafted piece has its own character, and the small variations that come with handmade work are part of what makes cane furniture special.
