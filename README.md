## How to Replace Images

When you add your real photos, replace these Unsplash placeholders:

| Location | Current URL | Replace With |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| Hero | `photo-1586281383665-3f2f49f7ebd9` | Your hero image (1920x1080) |
| About | `photo-1582719478246-dff070ce35e4` | Your workshop image |
| Chairs | `photo-1555022610-01df566e2639` | Your chair products |
| Swings | `photo-1554161029-5fa5999edbf0` | Your swing products |
| Tables | `photo-1582719478290-3f6e0e0290e1` | Your table/teapoy products |
| Décor | `photo-1594737625775-8a3fef5a4c9f` | Your decorative items |
| Baskets | `photo-1600585154340-be6161a56a0c` | Your basket/hampers |

Simply update the `src` attribute in `index.html`:
```html
<img src="images/your-photo.jpg" alt="Description of product" loading="lazy">
```
